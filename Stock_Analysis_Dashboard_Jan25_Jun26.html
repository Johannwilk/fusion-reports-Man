Key observations for the production team — Jan 2025 to Jun 2026 stock movement

GrowingPN 110 — stock build has plateaued at ~19,000–20,000 kg

Still the largest single contributor (+R1.31M). The aggressive build-up ran from Sep 2025 (3,600kg) to Mar 2026 (19,800kg) and has now levelled off — May 2026 held 19,800kg and June eased slightly to 19,200kg. Cost is stable at ~R71.21/kg. The build phase appears complete; the question for production is now whether ~19,000kg is the intended ongoing holding level, and what monthly consumption justifies it.

GrowingAPU Adhesive — June spike to R1.69M (15,000 units)

The biggest change in the new months: APU Adhesive jumped from R835k (7,000 units) in May to R1.69M (15,000 units) in June — a purchase of ~8,000 units in one month. Its pattern is highly lumpy: it peaked at R2.15M (18,000 units) in Oct 2025, drew down to 4,000 by Feb 2026, and has now been rebuilt. This "sawtooth" purchasing suggests large bulk orders; worth confirming whether volume discounts justify the carrying cost, and whether a smoother ordering cycle is feasible.

GrowingADH Hardener D — near doubling by June

Now the third-largest driver (+R803k). Grew steadily through 2025 and stepped up sharply in the final months to R855k by June 2026. Moves in tandem with APU Adhesive (they're companion products), so the June adhesive purchase likely explains this too.

New lineMELIO RESIN 719 — still volatile

Holding ~9,000–9,240kg (R590–610k) steadily since Mar 2026 after the erratic 2025 pattern (zero in Sep–Oct 2025, then a 10,000kg purchase in Nov). Cost settled at R65.52/kg, down from R87.58 initially. The last four months show a more stable holding — but 9,000kg still represents ~7 months of cover at current usage. Review order size.

New lineJul–Sep 2025 new product cluster — now embedded

The finishing/adhesive range introduced mid-2025 (PLUS TOP 600, MELIO PROMUL C-83, MELIO P-4893, EURODERM NERO NBE 420%, AQUALENE AKU, RU 400) is now a permanent R6.3M block of the stock holding. PLUS TOP 600 is cycling normally (3,000–5,000 units). This cluster is the single biggest structural reason stock is higher than in early 2025.

DecliningWS 640 — continuing steady drawdown

Down from R1.07M (7,990kg) in Jan 2025 to R750k (5,780kg) by June 2026 — a consistent decline of roughly 100–200kg per month with no restocking. At the current drawdown rate this remains one of the highest-value slow movers; months-of-cover is still around 12+ months. A candidate for supplier return or usage acceleration if the product is being phased out.

DiscontinuedRun-down block complete — R1.76M released

All 54 discontinued lines (including RU 175, EURODERM BRUNO DRB, PLUS OIL 07 and the TANCURT range) reached zero by June 2026. This released R1.76M of working capital — the offsetting saving against the new product additions. TANCURT M 507 and PN 500 still hold ~R70k each and are nearly depleted.
